# 📈 Retention Strategy & Social Loops — Orbit (Chaos)

This document establishes the user retention loops, triggers, and rewards engineered to sustain recurring usage across Day 1, Day 7, Day 30, and Day 90.

---

## 🎡 The Hook Model Integration

Orbit uses a continuous feedback loop to transition users from external push notifications to internal habit triggers.

```
       [1. TRIGGER]
       External: "Friend logged Ramen Fuel bill nearby."
       Internal: Anticipation of shared memory validation.
           |
           v
       [2. ACTION]
       Open Orbit, pan Shibuya grid, tap geolocated pin.
           |
           v
       [3. VARIABLE REWARD]
       View parallel photo perspectives / like count increases /
       spontaneous Chaos wheel spins.
           |
           v
       [4. INVESTMENT]
       Log an expense split, drop an inside joke pin, or post a memory.
```

## 🎯 Retention Milestones & Tactics

### 🏁 Day 1 Retention (The First 24 Hours)
- **Objective**: Guide users past the lock screen and introduce the Map OS layers.
- **Tactics**:
  - *Immersive Auth*: The lock screen biometric/passcode challenge creates a sense of entering an exclusive space.
  - *Orb Companion Tutorial*: The robotic guide explains features, suggesting a test split bill.
  - *Theme Morphing*: Allowing users to personalize visual and audio gradients creates immediate ownership.
- **Target D1 Metric**: **72%** active user retention.

### 📅 Day 7 Retention (The Weekly Rhythm)
- **Objective**: Establish weekly coordination routines.
- **Tactics**:
  - *Live Tracking & Signal Trails*: Visual indicators of friend movements create a natural curiosity loop to open the app.
  - *Spontaneous Chaos Mode*: Prompts groups to meet up over the weekend via roulette suggestions.
- **Target D7 Metric**: **48%** active user retention.

### 🗓️ Day 30 Retention (The Monthly Habit)
- **Objective**: Secure group-wide coordination.
- **Tactics**:
  - *Bill Settlement Loop*: Unsettled bill pins (`💸`) remain highlighted on the Shibuya canvas.
  - *Inside Jokes Accumulation*: A growing list of geolocated quotes provides a shared group history.
- **Target D30 Metric**: **35%** active user retention.

### 📆 Day 90 Retention (The Emotional Hub)
- **Objective**: Make the app the historical record of the friendship group.
- **Tactics**:
  - *Parallel Memories Timeline*: Friends open the app just to scroll back through shared trips.
  - *Memory Anniversaries*: Dynamic Island notifications prompt users to "rewind" to last year's Shibuya trip, re-igniting planning loops for the next planet.
- **Target D90 Metric**: **25%** active user retention.
