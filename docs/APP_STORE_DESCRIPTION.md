## 🌟 Long Description Text

**Welcome to Orbit (Chaos) — the social operating system for close friend groups.**

Orbit is not another dry checklist organizer or boring corporate spreadsheet tool. It is a living, breathing digital universe designed specifically for your close friends. Say goodbye to fragmented chat logs, lost coordinates, and forgotten photo streams. In Orbit, the entire interface is a geolocated Shibuya Map Canvas that centers your group’s shared memories, live location trails, and coordination inside a beautiful spatial universe.

We designed Orbit to feel alive. Every interaction features spring-loaded dynamics, customizable neon color schemes, and real-time synthesized audio chimes.

### Key Pillars:

* **MAP-AS-AN-OS**: No static tab structures. Toggle layer filters directly over a vector Shibuya canvas. Find where friends are wandering, track their incoming flights, and pin inside jokes to streets.
* **PARALLEL MEMORIES**: Pinned photo clusters geolocate moments. See late-night ramen runs from three different perspectives in one card.
* **SHARED EXPENSES**: Split bills on coordinates. Add expenses, designate splits, and view visual debt balances with beautiful balance bubbles directly on the map.
* **SPONTANEOUS CHAOS MODE**: Stuck in decision paralysis? Tap neon anomalies on the map to trigger the spontaneous roulette spinner and let the cosmos decide your karaoke spots or street food targets.
* **ORB AI COMPANION**: Your interactive floating guide that helps suggest routes, summarizes splits, and points out coordinate alerts.

### Designed for Apple ProMotion and iOS:
Experience fluids transitions running at a native 60 FPS (120 FPS on ProMotion devices). Select from six curated high-contrast dark themes to match your vibe.

**We live inside Chaos. Start orbiting today.**
