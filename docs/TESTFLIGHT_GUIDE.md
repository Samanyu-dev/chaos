# ✈️ TestFlight Beta Guide — Orbit (Chaos)

Welcome to the Orbit (Chaos) TestFlight Beta program! This guide helps you navigate our sandbox, understand what is simulated, and submit feedback to the engineering team.

---

## 📲 How to Install

1. Download **TestFlight** from the iOS App Store.
2. Click the invitation link received via email or redeem the public beta code.
3. Install **Orbit (Chaos)**.
4. Open the application and complete the splash screen transition.

---

## 🔎 Focus Areas for Beta Testers

We want you to test the stability and look-and-feel of our unified **Map-as-an-OS** shell:

### 1. Kinetic Transitions & Performance
- Tap through the bottom tab layers (**Home, Tracker, Memories, Chaos, Group**).
- Confirm that the camera pans and zooms cleanly to Shibuya coordinates without visual stutters.
- Toggle between active color themes in the Group profile layer settings drawer. Verify that particle meshes adapt instantly.

### 2. Geolocated Expenses
- Tap the **[📸 Memories]** tab, select the **Expenses** filter, and click an expense pin (`💸`).
- Tap the `+` button in the top HUD and log a new split bill. Verify that a new orange expense pin appears at Shibuya coordinates and splits balances correctly.

### 3. Ambient Audio & Haptics
- Ensure your iPhone ring switch is set to **unmuted**.
- Confirm that tactile clicks trigger on tab presses, and sonar chimes play when expenses are committed or pins are tapped.

---

## 📨 Submitting Feedback

If you encounter a crash, bug, or layout misalignment:
1. Take a screenshot in-app.
2. In the TestFlight screenshot menu, click **Share Beta Feedback**.
3. Detail the steps to replicate the issue, especially which color theme and tab layer were active when it occurred.
