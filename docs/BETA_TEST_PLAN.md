# 👥 Beta Test Plan — Cohort & Scenario Guide

This document designs a structured beta testing protocol to collect qualitative feedback and validation metrics from 40 target friend groups.

---

## 📈 Testing Cohorts Recruitment Roadmap

```
  PHASE 1 (Cohort A)           PHASE 2 (Cohort B)           PHASE 3 (Cohort C)
       5 Groups                     10 Groups                    25 Groups
  "Internal Alpha Circles"     "Target Travel Cohorts"      "Public Beta Clusters"
  Focus: Core crashes,         Focus: Retention,            Focus: Viral growth,
  sound checks, basic pans     split bills validation       organic invites loops
```

- **Cohort A (5 Groups)**: Close acquaintances and local development circles. Used to identify immediate performance glitches, frame drops, and lock-screen auth bypass errors.
- **Cohort B (10 Groups)**: Active travelers planning weekend outings or roommates tracking shared groceries. Focuses on the accuracy of geolocated bills and the timing profile of Chaos roulette suggestions.
- **Cohort C (25 Groups)**: University students, long-distance friend circles, and vacationing travelers recruited via social media. Focuses on organic invitation rates and virality coefficients.

---

## 🗺️ Structured Test Scenarios

Beta groups will execute one of five coordinate tracking test scripts:

### Scenario 1: The Weekend Road Trip
- **Cohort**: Groups of 3–5 friends taking a 2-day trip.
- **Task**: Track member arrivals on the Shibuya map tracker, log split bills for meals, and post at least 3 geolocated memory perspectives.
- **Feedback Metrics**: Do coordinates pan accurately? Is the split bill balance display clear?

### Scenario 2: College Friend Hangouts
- **Cohort**: Campus student groups.
- **Task**: Pin inside jokes to campus locations, drop reaction signals, and use the theme switcher to match the group's mood.
- **Feedback Metrics**: Do jokes pins capture the shared group history?

### Scenario 3: Roommates Shared OS
- **Cohort**: 2–4 housemates sharing rent and groceries.
- **Task**: Use the geolocated Expenses tab to split utility bills, groceries, and shared household items.
- **Feedback Metrics**: Does the interface serve daily splits as effectively as dedicated transactional trackers?

### Scenario 4: Long Distance Friends Link
- **Cohort**: Friends separated by cities or timezones.
- **Task**: Share status updates, track mock flight countdowns, and build a countdown schedule to their next reunion.
- **Feedback Metrics**: Does the app reduce the feeling of distance?

### Scenario 5: Travel Planning Cockpit
- **Cohort**: Friends planning a future international vacation.
- **Task**: Generate a custom Trip Planet, build the itinerary card deck, and vote on activities in the agenda cards.
- **Feedback Metrics**: Does the planning UI build excitement?
