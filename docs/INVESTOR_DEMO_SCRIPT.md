# 🎤 5-Minute Investor Demo Script — Orbit (Chaos)

This pitch script outlines a 5-minute, high-fidelity narrative demo of Orbit (Chaos) designed for accelerators, investors, and startup pitch competitions.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Section Breakdown

| Section | Duration | Key Message | Visual State |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Hook & Problem** | 60 sec | Social tools are sterile transactional utilities; relationships are fragmented. | Splash Screen and Onboarding Slide |
| **2. The Solution: Map-as-an-OS**| 90 sec | Introducing a spatial operating system where the group lives inside Shibuya coordinates. | Map OS Core Layer (`.home`) |
| **3. Friendship Engine & Memories**| 60 sec | Parallel memory rewinds and geolocated split bills. | Memories / Expenses Filters Layer |

---

## 📖 Script Walkthrough

### 🎬 Part 1: The Hook & The Problem (0:00 - 1:00)
- **Speaker**: "Look at the social apps on your phone. Messaging threads, transaction splitters, photo grids. They are sterile, transactional utilities. They help us coordinate, but they don't capture the feeling of being together. We are more connected than ever, yet our friendships are fragmented. Friend groups don't want another tool. They want a space. They want a social operating system."
- **Visual Action**: Boot the application on-screen. Let the cinematic starfields of the **Splash Screen** load, playing the PCM low-pass transition sweep as you enter the onboarding gates.

---

### 🎨 Part 2: The Solution & Map-as-an-OS (1:00 - 2:30)
- **Speaker**: "Meet Orbit. A social operating system for close friends. Our design philosophy is simple: **We live inside Chaos, we don't just use it**. We've replaced tabs and menus with a unified Map OS. The map isn't a feature; the entire application *is* the map."
- **Visual Action**: Enter the passcode `1997` to bypass the lock screen. The Shibuya Map canvas zooms and centers on the **Trip Planet** floating in space. Swipe up the bottom directory drawer.
- **Speaker**: "Here is our Shibuya trip. We see the days left, our flight countdown widgets, and our upcoming itinerary. When I tap the Tracker layer, the camera pans. I see exactly where my friends are. I can tap Kai's node to view his trail, send a signal reaction, and watch neon bubbles float up."

---

### 📸 Part 3: The Friendship Engine (2:30 - 3:30)
- **Speaker**: "Orbit is built around the **Friendship Engine**. We anchor relationships to coordinates. Let's look at the Memories layer. When Lila and I visited Shibuya Crossing, we captured it from different angles. In Orbit, those photos aren't lost in a chat feed. They are geolocated together. I can swipe to see Lila's photo, and then swipe again to see Kai's perspective of the exact same moment."
- **Visual Action**: Tap the Memories tab, switch the segment to **Expenses**, and click an orange bill pin (`💸`).
- **Speaker**: "Even expenses are spatial. We split ramen bill offsets here. I can log splits, and the app displays balances directly over the map canvas."

---

### 🎰 Part 4: Chaos Mode (3:30 - 4:30)
- **Speaker**: "But we also want to break real-world decision paralysis. Tap the Chaos layer. You will see glowing anomalies indicating nearby adventures. Tapping one initiates a roulette spin."
- **Visual Action**: Tap the Akihabara anomaly. Tap **Initiate Spin**. Watch the roulette decelerate with chiptune sound ticks, concluding with victory audio.
- **Speaker**: "Instead of arguing about where to go, Chaos decides. It suggests a local karaoke spot, prompting the group to head out."

---

### 🚀 Part 5: The Business Model & Vision (4:30 - 5:00)
- **Speaker**: "We're launching on TestFlight with this core Shibuya template. Our roadmap includes integrating CoreLocation, Apple ActivityKit widgets, and native visionOS maps. We aren't building a utility app. We're building a spatial home for friend groups. Thank you."
- **Visual Action**: Transition back to Settings and slide out the logout overlay.
