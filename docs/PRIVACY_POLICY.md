# 🔒 Privacy Policy — Orbit (Chaos)

Last Updated: June 5, 2026

Chaos Social, Inc. ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Orbit mobile application. This Privacy Policy details how we handle location offsets, shared financial inputs, uploaded media files, and anonymous usage telemetry.

---

## 1. Data Collection & Usage

### 🗺️ Location Data (Coordinates)
Orbit uses background location signals to update friend positions on the Shibuya Map canvas.
- **Precision**: Background locations are transformed into general map offset coordinates (`latOffset` and `lonOffset`) relative to Shibuya, Tokyo. We do not store precise GPS coordinates on external servers.
- **Retention**: Location offsets are stored in local device memory and cleared upon session termination.

### 📸 Memories & Uploaded Media
Photos, custom captions, and sender avatars uploaded to the geolocated memory pins are stored securely. These are accessible only to authorized members of your specific group.

### 💳 Expenses & Split Data
Expense descriptions, transaction categories, and billing splits logged inside the App are stored to track group balances. We do not integrate with bank logins or process actual credit card transactions.

---

## 2. Analytics Telemetry
To maintain performance and diagnostic stability, we collect anonymous usage telemetry:
- Device model and iOS operating system version.
- Screen transition frequencies and UI theme selections.
- Average response durations of the synthesized audio engine.
- This telemetry is completely isolated from personal usernames or names.

---

## 3. Data Protection
Your data is protected by the native iOS sandbox environment. All local cache records are encrypted using AES-256 standards when the device is locked.
